Privacy Policy for ShareLoc

Last updated: October 15, 2026

ShareLoc is a location-sharing web application that allows users to share their live location with people they choose via a link.

Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture to identify your account.
  • Location Data: If you choose to share your location, your device location is used to generate a shareable link. Location data is shared only with people you send the link to.

How We Use Information

  • To provide sign-in and identify users.
  • To display your location to people you explicitly share it with.
  • To improve the service.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your location is only visible to anyone who has the link you shared. You can stop sharing at any time by closing the sharing session.

Data Storage

Location data is used in real-time for sharing purposes and is not used for advertising.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Sign-In for authentication. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, contact us at: https://shareloc.is-a.dev/
